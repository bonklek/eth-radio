Station operations and economics

Proposed safety boundaries for funding, allocation, sponsorship, and policy—not a live market or vault.

PLANNED / EXPERIMENTAL
DocumentationProtocol modelPublisher safety

There is no deployed Station V2 vault, auction, advertising module, reimbursement claim, or revenue-routing interface documented here. Do not send funds or make an economic decision from this proposed model.

Funding and vault safety

EXPERIMENTAL The constitution schema can represent a vault balance, protected reserve, total liabilities, withdrawal path, and revenue relationship. It emits critical warnings for insolvency, an unfunded protected reserve, general administrative withdrawal, or commingled protected funding. It is an inspection schema, not a deployed vault.

PLANNED A future Station funding model should distinguish free funds from protected publication funding, hard/conditional assignments, mature liabilities, and pull-based claims. A Station operator must not be able to treat protected publication funding as general revenue. A name failure, policy change, or tombstone should not erase an already-established claim—but no contract currently provides this guarantee.

Runway and capacity

Runway is only an estimate. It depends on current/guarded/stress fees, media density, policy limits, confirmed liabilities, retained reserves, and a chosen horizon. It is not a balance, a promise to publish, a capacity reservation, or a guarantee that blobs remain retrievable. Displaying an estimate without its inputs can be misleading.

Allocation and airtime

PLANNED Fixed-lot auctions, bid increments, self-outbids, overlap protection, admission caps, cancellation, release, and related-party disclosures all require explicit public policy. A client fee snapshot cannot set an auction floor, settle a bid, reserve capacity, or authorize reimbursement.

Advertising and sponsorship

Proposed formSafety boundary
Dedicated interstitial or dead-air assetWould need disclosed canonical inventory, eligibility, frequency limits, revenue routing, and separate policy. It is not currently deployed.
Creator-integrated sponsorshipContent can be sponsored before publication, but the protocol cannot independently prove an impression or enforce a commercial agreement.
Client-side overlay adOptional presentation only. Users can remove or modify it, so it must not be sold as a guaranteed impression or canonical revenue event.
Fallback advertisingWould require a bounded policy that avoids an endless ad loop and never labels fallback as a live primary.

“Canonical opportunity” and “observed impression” must remain separate. A proposed scheduled opportunity can be evidenced by future policy/state; a particular client observation depends on playback, provider availability, and client behavior.

Policy and governance boundaries

PLANNED Separate policy modules may eventually govern publishing authorization, scheduling, allocation, fallback, fees, funding, advertising, or revenue. Mutable modules, unknown interfaces, unsupported profiles, and policy transitions should be exposed as warnings rather than hidden. Ownership, publishing, funding, and scheduling capability must remain separate; a token, NFT, multisig, or DAO rule is not automatically safe or immutable.

Before relying on a Station economy

  • Verify a released contract, its deployment, chain, interface/version, policy module, and applicable audits.
  • Read the exact fee, refund, claim, cancellation, and change policy before sending funds or bidding.
  • Check whether protected reserves and liabilities are independently visible and whether any general withdrawal path exists.
  • Treat all current pages as design education until the required public interfaces and reviews exist.
