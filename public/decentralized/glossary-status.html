Glossary

Station V1 VERIFIED
A V1 contract that emits bounded segment-publication events. A proposed V2 Station is a separate identity/model and is not deployed by this documentation.
Channel V1 / PLANNED V2
In V1, a publisher-scoped stream within a Station. V2 proposes a separately derived channel ID and channel generation; do not merge histories based on name text.
Segment / Blob V1 VERIFIED
A bounded media unit and the blob data referenced by its Station event. The watcher verifies matching sidecars and payload hashes before playback.
BPO OPEN
No released public source defines this acronym or its lifecycle. Treat it as an unresolved term, not an action, claim, or API.
Reservation / hard assignment PLANNED / OPEN
A reservation is a proposed lot/program allocation. A hard assignment is not a released V1 or V2 contract concept here; it must not be presented as protected funding or a guaranteed slot.
Claim / runway PLANNED / OPEN
A claim is a proposed pull-based liability; runway is a proposed estimate of how long funds may support policy-defined activity. Neither currently creates a payable right or reliable duration.
Season / turnover EXPERIMENTAL / PLANNED
A season schema divides a block range into non-overlapping lots. Turnover is a proposed refresh period between seasons; it is not a V1 scheduler or clock guarantee.
Scheduled replay / standing fallback PLANNED
Scheduled replay is intended as assigned playback. Standing fallback is intended as a transactionless client selection from committed assets. Neither proves a primary stream is live.
Canonical plan / observed selection EXPERIMENTAL
Proposed separation between chain-backed policy/evidence and the media a client actually obtained through its providers. Provider observations must not silently rewrite canonical order.
`.gwei` receipt / policy module PLANNED
A `.gwei` receipt is not a released identity or payment primitive. A policy module is a proposed, separately verifiable source of authorization, economics, fallback, or turnover behavior.
Publication floor / inclusion feasibility OPEN
These are not defined public interfaces. A client fee forecast cannot establish either a minimum policy threshold or transaction inclusion.

How to use this matrix

  • Use shipped-and-verified terms only for current V1 behavior you can confirm from the contract and watcher.
  • Show experimental schema versions and unsupported states to developers; do not infer a future default.
  • Use planned language for proposed flows, then require a direct address/transaction recovery path until production evidence exists.
  • Stop and seek a released specification when an open term would affect money, authority, availability, or user safety.
